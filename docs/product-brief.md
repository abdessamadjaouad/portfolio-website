immediate availability, work arrangements, three strongest experience
highlights, both resumes, and the preferred contact path.

### Engineering manager

Within three minutes, an engineering manager can distinguish Abdessamad's
personal contribution from team or employer context, see resume-backed

### Senior engineer

A senior engineer can inspect concise, sanitized architecture summaries,

invented implementation details or inaccessible private links. Deeper technical
material is limited to approved personal projects and research evidence.

## Content principles

- Lead with evidence rather than decorative claims.
- Keep Data Engineer evidence before Software Engineer evidence.
- Use only approved facts and resume-backed responsibilities, technologies,
  dates, education, certifications, languages, and results.
- Mark genuine unresolved fields with a named `TODO_CONTENT_*` marker.
- Keep employer highlights separate from personal projects.
- Never imply that an internship was full-time employment.
- Do not publish internal source code, data, screenshots, repositories, reports,
  credentials, schemas, topology, endpoints, hostnames, logos, or workflows.
- Do not add skill bars, fake counters, testimonials, client logos, or invented
  metrics.
